Northwind Logistics Employee Handbook. Welcome to Northwind Logistics. This handbook explains how we work together. It applies to every permanent employee in the Durban, Cape Town and Gqeberha offices. Contractors should follow the separate supplier code of conduct.
Your first week. New starters meet their buddy on day one and complete the safety induction by the end of day two. Laptops are issued by the IT desk on the third floor. Access cards are collected from reception and must be worn visibly in the warehouse.
Probation lasts for three months. Your manager will hold a review meeting at the end of each month during that period.

Annual leave. Full-time staff receive twenty-two working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time staff receive a pro rata amount. Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year, and carried days expire on 31 March.
Requests for more than five consecutive days must be submitted at least three weeks ahead through the PeopleDesk portal.
Sick leave. Employees may take up to thirty days of paid sick leave in a three-year cycle. A doctor's certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days.
Parental leave. Birth mothers receive four months of paid maternity leave. Other parents receive ten consecutive days of paid parental leave.
=== page ===
Remote work. Employees may work from home up to two days a week with their manager's approval. Wednesday is an office day for everyone, because team meetings happen then. Warehouse and dispatch roles are not eligible for remote work.
Home office allowance. After probation, remote-eligible staff may claim a once-off allowance of R3,500 for a desk or chair.
Core hours. Whatever your location, you must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 on working days.
=== page ===
Travel and expenses. Claims must be submitted through PeopleDesk within thirty days of the expense, with a photo of each receipt. Claims above R5,000 need director approval. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at R4.84 per kilometre.
Meals while travelling are capped at R450 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
Grievances. If you have a complaint, raise it with your manager first. If that is not appropriate, email the confidential ethics line at speakup@northwind.example, which is read only by the head of compliance.
