Seabreeze Municipality Household Waste and Recycling Guide. This guide explains what to put in each bin and when your waste is collected. Every household receives one green bin for general waste and one blue bag allocation for recycling.
Collection days. Northern suburbs are collected on Tuesdays and southern suburbs on Thursdays. Put your bins out by 06:30 and bring them in the same evening. When a public holiday falls on a collection day, collection moves to the following Saturday.

What goes in the blue bag. Clean paper and cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastics numbered 1, 2 and 5. Rinse containers first. Flatten cardboard boxes.
What stays out. Polystyrene, nappies, garden waste, broken window glass and ceramics cannot be recycled through kerbside collection. Greasy pizza boxes belong in the green bin.
Replacement blue bags are free from any library or municipal office. Each household may collect up to ten bags a month.
=== page ===
Garden waste and large items. Garden refuse may be taken free of charge to the Kingfisher Drop-off Centre, which is open from 07:00 to 17:00 every day except Sunday. Trailers are limited to one load per day. Bulky household items such as old mattresses are collected on request for a fee of R180 per item.
Hazardous waste. Batteries, paint, fluorescent tubes and motor oil must never go in a bin. Bring them to the hazardous waste day held on the first Saturday of every month at the Harbour Road depot. Electronic waste is accepted at the same event.
=== page ===
Fines and reporting. Illegal dumping carries a fine of up to R15,000. Report dumping or a missed collection through the Seabreeze Connect app or by calling 086 000 4422. Missed collections must be reported within 48 hours.
Composting. Residents can buy a subsidised compost bin for R250 from the Kingfisher centre. Vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and dry leaves compost well. Do not add meat, dairy or cooked food, because they attract rats.
